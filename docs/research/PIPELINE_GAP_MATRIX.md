## Decision rule for gaps

Missing external evidence remains `BLOCKED_EXTERNAL`; it is not replaced by a fixture, an LLM response, or a simulated measurement. Deterministic schema, algorithm and exporter work can continue independently and must retain `REVIEW_REQUIRED`, `UNKNOWN`, `UNRESOLVED`, `NOT_MEASURED` and `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE` where appropriate.
